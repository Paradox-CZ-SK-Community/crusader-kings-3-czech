## Reuse the existing FirstName and TitleAsName declension.
## ShortUIName follows the existing ShortUINamePossessive composition:
## title + regnal first name. NotMe never substitutes the player pronoun.
## Player branches intentionally retain their original getter output.
## UIName preserves any remaining dynasty/nickname/territory text as-is.
## UIName falls back to its original text if its prefix cannot be matched.
